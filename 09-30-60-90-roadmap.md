# 09. 30-60-90 Day Deployment Roadmap

A first-90-days plan for leading a Copilot program from approval to an interim pilot read-out, with the first scale decision at about day 120. It assumes licenses have been budgeted, the tenant is on Microsoft 365 E3 or E5, and nothing has been rolled out widely yet.

The 90 days end with **evidence and a checkpoint**, not a full rollout. The scale decision follows at about day 120, once the pilot has run its full measured window. Oversharing remediation across the whole tenant often takes longer than 90 days. That is why the pilot runs only on content that has already been fixed.

---

## Days 1 to 30: Mobilize and assess

**Goal:** governance in place, readiness known, Wave 1 candidates chosen.

| Workstream | Activities | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Governance | Charter the AI Governance Committee and AI Review Group; confirm decision rights and the RACI ([05](05-governance-model.md)) | Signed charter |
| Policy | Adapt and approve the [acceptable-use standard](reference/acceptable-use-standard.md) | Approved standard |
| Intake | Run Phase 0 intake sessions with 6 to 10 department leaders ([02](02-phase-0-opportunity-intake.md)) | 15 to 25 intake records |
| Readiness | Complete the nine-domain assessment ([03](03-readiness-assessment.md)); run SharePoint Advanced Management and Purview oversharing reports on the top 100 sites | Readiness ratings and remediation plan |
| Interim controls | Restricted Content Discovery on high-risk sites; DLP for Copilot in simulation; audit on | Interim control record |
| Prioritization | Score candidates; agree Wave 1 ([04](04-prioritization-model.md)) | Ranked list; 2 to 4 Wave 1 use cases |
| Stakeholders | Stakeholder map; executive and manager messaging drafted | Communications plan |

**Day 30 gate (CTO):** readiness go/no-go for the Wave 1 scope.

## Days 31 to 60: Baseline and launch

**Goal:** baselines locked, pilot running with support.

| Workstream | Activities | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Pull 8 weeks of historical data from operational systems where it exists; timed samples where it does not; pick a comparison group | Locked baseline report |
| Approvals | Tier decisions, conditions, success and stop criteria for each Wave 1 use case | Approval records |
| Remediation | Fix permissions and ownership on the sites that ground Wave 1 use cases; move DLP from simulation to enforcement | Remediated sites list |
| Training | Safe-use module, fundamentals, role labs, manager briefing | Training completion of 100% before access |
| Champions | Recruit and train champions for each pilot team | Champion roster |
| Launch | Assign licenses to the pilot cohort; open office hours and the Teams channel; publish starter prompts | Pilot live |
| Monitoring | Weekly adoption check; first pulse survey; incident log | Week 2 and week 4 check-ins |

**Day 60 checkpoint (AI Review Group):** adoption on track, no unresolved Sev 1 or Sev 2 incidents, early fixes made.

## Days 61 to 90: Run and read out

**Goal:** the pilot running at full strength, early evidence reviewed, and nothing scaled yet.

The pilot launches around day 35, so by day 90 it has finished its 2-week ramp and about 6 weeks of measured use. That is enough for an interim read-out but not for the scale decision: the pilot standard in [06](06-pilot-model.md) calls for 6 to 10 measured weeks, and outcome metrics such as handle time need the full window.

| Workstream | Activities | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Measurement | Interim read-out of the three layers against baseline and comparison group ([08](08-metrics-framework.md)) | Interim results |
| Quality and risk | QA sampling results, DLP trends, incident review | Guardrail summary |
| Course correction | Fix content gaps, prompts, and training issues found so far; apply stop criteria if triggered | Change log |
| Wave 2 | Re-score backlog after remediation; start Wave 2 intake and readiness | Wave 2 shortlist |
| Reporting | First monthly executive scorecard; first quarterly board risk committee summary | Scorecard and board summary |

**Day 90 gate (CTO and business owners):** continue, adjust, or stop each Wave 1 use case on the interim evidence; confirm the date of the scale decision.

## Days 91 to 120: Decide

**Goal:** an evidence-based decision on each Wave 1 use case, and a funded plan for what comes next.

| Workstream | Activities | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Measurement | Final results across the full measured window ([08](08-metrics-framework.md)) | Pilot results report |
| Decision | Scale, extend, redesign, or stop for each use case ([06](06-pilot-model.md)) | Decision record |
| Scale plan | Wave plan by department, license allocation, champion coverage, training calendar | Scale plan |
| Operate | Standing intake cadence; license reclamation rule; semi-annual standard review date set | Operating calendar |

**Day 120 gate (CTO and business owners):** scale decision and funding for the next wave.

---

## What leadership sees at each gate

| Gate | Questions answered |
|---|---|
| Day 30 | Are we safe to pilot? Which problems are we solving first, and who owns them? |
| Day 60 | Are people using it? Is anything going wrong? |
| Day 90 | Is the early evidence on track? What needs fixing before the final read-out? |
| Day 120 | Did the business measurably improve? What do we scale, fix, or stop? What comes next? |

## Common risks in the first 90 days

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Oversharing remediation runs late | Limit pilot grounding to remediated sites; Restricted Content Discovery on the rest |
| No baseline data for a use case | Move it to Wave 2; do not pilot without a baseline |
| Pilot cohort limited to enthusiasts | Require a mix of skeptics and average performers |
| Executive pressure to license everyone at once | Show the day-120 decision gate and the cost of unused licenses |
| Workforce anxiety | Clear message on how released time will be used; involve frontline champions |
